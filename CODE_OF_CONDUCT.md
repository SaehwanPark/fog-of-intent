# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Participants in Fog of Intent are expected to be respectful, constructive, and
specific. We welcome disagreement about design and implementation when it is
focused on evidence, constraints, and the work. Harassment, discrimination,
threats, personal attacks, deliberate intimidation, sexualized conduct, and
publishing another person's private information without permission are not
acceptable.

## Scope

This policy applies to project spaces, pull requests, issues, discussions,
reviews, and project-related communication. Maintainers may also act on conduct
that materially disrupts project participation or threatens contributor safety.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report a conduct concern privately by emailing
[`saehwan.simon.park@gmail.com`](mailto:saehwan.simon.park@gmail.com). Include
enough context to investigate and avoid forwarding sensitive material more
widely. For a security vulnerability, use the same address and do not disclose
the details publicly until the maintainers have acknowledged the report.

Maintainers will review reports as promptly and confidentially as practical,
protect the reporter where possible, and may edit or remove harmful content,
request a correction, restrict participation, or close a contribution. A
maintainer who is directly involved in a report should not be the sole decision
maker for it.

This policy is a project participation standard, not legal advice. The project
does not promise a particular outcome or timeline for a report.
